## Prompt 摘要

### logo-primary

Premium static brand identity logo for “创智学院” with “SII”, abstract intelligent emergence symbol, open geometric field, nodes, subtle C/Z gesture, luminous knowledge core, deep navy background, quantum cyan and violet accents, polished bilingual lockup, no robots, brains, circuit boards, shields, books, or cheap neon.

### logo-horizontal

Premium horizontal bilingual logo lockup for “创智学院 / Shanghai Innovation Institute”, left abstract static SII intelligence-field mark, right readable Chinese and English text, institutional and international research institute style, clean typography, scalable mark, avoid school badges and tech cliches.

### color-palette

Static brand color palette presentation board with labeled swatches and hex codes, Swiss grid, scientific precision, generous whitespace, subtle node-grid background, bilingual title.

### typography-specimen

Premium typography specimen board with Chinese headline “创智学院”, tagline “从不可能到可能，从可能到价值”, English heading, SII abbreviation, hierarchy examples, modern geometric sans-serif, strong grid, restrained scientific visual language.

### brand-mockup

High-end static brand application mockup showing building sign, conference poster, ID badge, laptop sticker, tote bag, presentation slide, and website hero card, coherent visual identity in deep navy, white, cyan, violet, silver gray, and small orange accent.

### individual mockups

第二轮将综合 mockup 拆分为单物品生成，分别覆盖楼宇门头、通用证件、笔记本贴纸、帆布袋、官网首屏。所有 prompt 均明确禁止真实姓名、人像、电话、邮箱、地址、二维码、条码、证件号、学号、登录信息或可识别个人信息。

### academy posters
