# Asset Generation Plan

## Rationale
The brief positions 创智学院 / Shanghai Innovation Institute as an AI frontier talent and research-entrepreneurship accelerator, not a conventional campus brand. This run should therefore build a restrained but high-signal digital identity system that can hold research news, open-source releases, recruitment CTAs, and selective admissions-camp communications without falling into school clichés.

## Visual Direction
- Selected: research-signal
- Why: Section 5 repeatedly translates the organization into signal lines, scanning grids, coordinate points, node networks, compute matrices, and open frontier frames; Section 7 names “前沿信号、共创网络、AI 基础设施、算力矩阵、冷静高能” as core keywords.
- What this prevents: This avoids a default education/campus direction built around shields, books, campus buildings, brochures, or student-life imagery.

## Deliverable Strategy
- Selected: digital-system
- Why: Section 11 ranks the website homepage/news card system, admissions-camp templates, research/open-source release templates, and talent recruitment visuals as Top priority. These are all recurring digital communications that need a unified visual grammar more than a one-off campaign poster or spatial rollout.
