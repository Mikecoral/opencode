# Brand Tokens: 创智学院（Shanghai Innovation Institute）

## Color Palette
| Role | Name | Hex | Usage |
|------|------|-----|-------|
| Primary | Deep Compute Blue | #071A3A | Logo, dark UI fields, important titles, institutional authority |
| Accent | Signal Cyan | #00D4FF | CTAs, signal lines, data highlights, interaction states |
| Surface | Paper White | #F7F9FC | Information-dense backgrounds, website modules, research cards |
| Muted | Muted Grey | #8A93A3 | Secondary labels, metadata, graph lines, captions |

**Accent discipline:** one accent color only. Signal Cyan is the active accent for this run; Frontier Violet and Marveller Orange from the brief are intentionally not used in Phase 2 assets to preserve a restrained research identity.

## Typography
| Role | Style Description | Weight Range |
|------|-------------------|-------------|
| Display / Logo | Rational modern Chinese sans / humanist-geometric hybrid Latin sans, widened or variable-width headline behavior, engineered but not cold | Bold / Black |
| Body | Clear open-aperture modern sans-serif for high-density technical information | Regular / Medium |
| Caption | Compatible sans-serif with precise numerals and metadata rhythm | Light / Regular |
