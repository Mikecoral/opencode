# Brief Review: 创智学院 / Shanghai Innovation Institute

## Overall Brief Quality: 8.2/10

## Section Scores
| Section | Score | Issue |
|---------|-------|-------|
| Design Keywords | 8/10 | Strong strategic vocabulary, but several terms are abstract and need visual translation rules to avoid generic AI grids. |
| Visual Direction | 9/10 | Clear “Intelligent Grid” concept with concrete references; could specify what to borrow versus avoid from each reference. |
| Color Strategy | 8/10 | Specific hues and roles are provided, but the blue/cyan/green/orange stack risks too many accents unless hierarchy is tightened. |
| Symbol & Mark | 8.5/10 | Distinctive modular mark direction and good avoid-list; final geometry is still exploratory rather than a single generation-ready mark recipe. |
| Typography | 7.5/10 | Good category guidance, but the font list is broad; image generation would benefit from one explicit Chinese/Latin pairing and spacing behavior. |

## Flagged Issues
- **Design Keywords:** “智能格局,” “研究操作系统,” and “可生成结构” are strategically strong but visually abstract. Suggested rewrite: “7×7 modular intelligence grid, lit path nodes, research-system diagrams, Shanghai West Bund dark infrastructure mood, disciplined institutional typography, youth-energy orange used only as signal.”
- **Color Strategy:** The palette defines one primary blue plus cyan, green, and orange, which may produce multi-accent technology visuals. Suggested rewrite: “Use deep navy/white/gray for 80% of the composition; use Innovation Blue as the only recurring accent; reserve cyan for thin connection lines and orange for one recruitment CTA only; avoid gradients unless they are restrained node-light effects.”
- **Symbol & Mark:** The brief offers multiple exploration paths but not one dominant mark construction. Suggested rewrite: “Create a master mark from an 8×8 grid: a geometric abstraction of 创/智 formed by 12–16 square modules and one diagonal/open-path cut, readable as a node-path ‘frontier gate’; no brain, circuit, chip, book, graduation cap, or chatbot shapes.”
- **Typography:** Typography names several possible families and does not fully define hierarchy. Suggested rewrite: “Use Source Han Sans/Noto Sans CJK SC in SemiBold/Bold for Chinese display; pair with IBM Plex Sans for Latin and numerals; use tight display tracking, tracked-out small uppercase English labels, and a restrained 4-step scale.”

## Recommendation
PROCEED — The brief is specific and distinctive enough for visual generation, with only minor tightening needed around accent discipline, final mark construction, and typography pairing.
