- **Readability Risks**:
  - The integration of graphical elements might distract from the text if not balanced carefully.
  - Over-reliance on geometric type can reduce warmth and the perception of accessibility.
- **Recommendation**: Best for forward-thinking, modern branding. It communicates innovation and flexibility, ideal for younger audiences or tech-centric settings.

---

  1. Ensure clear hierarchy in typography by balancing geometric headline with humanist secondary fonts for accessibility.
  2. Avoid overwhelming users with graphics and ensure adequate whitespace.
