# Concept Selection: 创智学院 / Shanghai Innovation Institute

## Confirmed Direction

- Logo: Use concept A as the core direction, but improve the clarity of the Chinese character structure. The mark should remain modular and geometric while making the relationship to “创智学院” more legible, especially the “创 / 智” logic.
- Color: Use a B + C hybrid. Research Light provides the everyday white / cloud-gray information environment; Recruitment Energy adds deep navy, innovation blue, cyan linework, and tightly controlled orange CTA accents.
- Typography / System: Use concept C first. Build around a node-path grid system with energetic but controlled visual rhythm, while preserving institutional readability.

## Generation Notes

- Preserve Chinese-first identity; English is secondary.
- Avoid generic AI symbols: no brain, chip, chatbot, graduation cap, or stock circuit-board imagery.
- Make the primary mark clearer than the exploration image: modular, ownable, and readable as a Chinese institutional identity rather than an abstract tech glyph.
- Orange should appear only as tactical recruitment / CTA signal, not as a dominant brand color.
