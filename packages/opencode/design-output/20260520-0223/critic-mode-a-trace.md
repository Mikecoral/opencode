## Rewrite Suggestions

### Design Keywords — Proposed Rewrite
1. **8×8 智能格局** — modular grid that generates marks, icons, and layouts.
2. **前沿问题现场** — research frontier as an active site, not a classroom.
3. **研究操作系统** — structured pathways connecting talent, mentors, compute, data, and industry scenes.
4. **青年研究者动能** — disciplined but energetic, with one bright signal color.
5. **节点—路径—阵地** — visible system of nodes, routes, and research fields.
6. **算力 / 数据基础设施** — deep technical base, dark navy, precise diagrams.
7. **开放协同网络** — universities, enterprises, labs, and founders as connected modules.
8. **上海西岸创新语境** — metropolitan research platform, not generic online learning.
9. **严肃机构 × 高能实验** — public credibility plus exploratory momentum.
10. **从未解问题到真实价值** — transformation from research challenge to social/industrial impact.

### Visual Direction — Proposed Rewrite
Use **Intelligent Grid** as the single master system. Borrow MIT Media Lab’s generative modular logic, IBM’s restrained grid/data discipline, The New School’s confident institutional typography, and Dropbox’s combinatorial form language — but do not imitate their marks or color systems. Visuals should show an 8×8 modular field where selected nodes form paths, research zones, and a scalable symbol family. The mood is dark-infrastructure, precise, research-grade, with measured white space and one bright blue signal.

### Color Strategy — Proposed Rewrite
Use a disciplined four-layer palette: **80% neutral infrastructure** (`#07111F`, `#FFFFFF`, `#F4F7FA`, `#DDE3EA`, `#8A96A8`, `#161616`), **8% primary accent** Innovation Blue (`#0F62FE`), **2% effect accent** Data Cyan (`#00A3FF`) only for thin connection lines or node glints, and **one-use event accent** Spark Orange (`#FF7A1A`) only for recruitment CTAs/open-day moments. AI Green (`#18B99A`) should be semantic only for progress/completion and not part of the main brand expression. Avoid large gradients, rainbow node networks, and purple-blue default tech effects.

### Symbol & Mark — Proposed Rewrite
Create the master mark from an **8×8 square grid**. Abstract the structure of **“创 / 智”** into 12–16 filled modules plus one diagonal or open-path cut, suggesting a frontier gate, intelligence node, and research pathway. The mark must work as a one-color favicon, social avatar, certificate seal, PPT corner bug, and signage mark. Generate a related icon family by changing which grid modules are activated for research fields such as cognitive intelligence, embodied intelligence, scientific intelligence, AI infrastructure, and AI safety. Do not use brains, circuits, chips, chatbot bubbles, books, graduation caps, rockets, red-gold seals, or mascots.

### Typography — Proposed Rewrite
Use **Source Han Sans / Noto Sans CJK SC** for Chinese display and body, paired with **IBM Plex Sans** for English, numerals, and interface labels. Chinese headlines use SemiBold/Bold with tight, confident spacing; body text uses Regular/Medium with open line height. English all-caps labels use +0.06–0.1em letter spacing; large display type uses slight negative tracking. Maintain a restrained type scale of 4–6 sizes and no more than two font families across the system. Optional variable width may be used only for research-direction labels, not for core readability.
