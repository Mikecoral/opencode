## Step 3 — Craft Evaluation Reasoning
### Color
Observed palette: deep navy `#07111F`/similar, white and cloud gray backgrounds, innovation blue route lines, cyan node accents, occasional green in system documentation, and orange only for CTA. Estimated proportions across the system remain neutral-dominant, generally 70–90% neutral/base, with accent usage highest on route-heavy campaign graphics. The accent repetition warning applies most to the forum and open-day compositions, but the repeated blue/cyan nodes are part of the mark grammar rather than a hard blocker.

### Typography
Chinese display typography is bold, high-contrast, and generally tightly set. English name is now correct in the regenerated forum poster. ALL CAPS is limited to acronym use such as AI, not long untracked labels. More than one typographic mood appears: modern sans across most assets, serif for forum/annual report. This is credible but slightly less unified with the brief’s technical sans recommendation.

### Hierarchy
Primary brand marks are now top- or edge-anchored across assets. The dashboard has a simple top bar and three large route bands, preventing overload. The admissions and open-day assets have a clear large headline and CTA hierarchy. The forum poster has strong headline hierarchy but the left-side icon/value column introduces a secondary visual language that is less ownable than the modular grid system.

The brief calls for a research operating system, intelligent grid, credible AI talent institution, and youth energy constrained by system logic. The corrected naming improves the score. The Chinese-first hierarchy is persuasive and legible, while Latin support is clean. The formal serif direction on the forum and annual report is tasteful but not fully integrated with the specified modern technical sans personality.

### Application Coherence — 8.4/10
The main coherence gap is fixed: dashboard and forum now use the same brand identity logic as the rest of the set. The applications feel like one institution across digital, event, print, certificate, and wayfinding. Minor secondary-language drift remains in pictorial side icons and serif formal materials.
