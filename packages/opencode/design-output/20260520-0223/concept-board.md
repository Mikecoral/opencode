## Option descriptions

### 1. Logo direction

**A — “创 / 智” modular abstraction**  
Best if the institute wants a more ownable Chinese-primary identity. It roots the mark in the official name while staying geometric and scalable. The risk is legibility if too much character structure is preserved, so finalization should simplify aggressively.

**B — 8×8 intelligent grid**  
Best for a long-term generative identity system. It directly supports sub-brand icons for research directions, camps, courses, certificates, and events. This is the strongest match to the brief’s “research operating system” and MIT Media Lab-style extensibility.

**C — Node–path–field symbol**  
Best for communicating ecosystem orchestration: students, mentors, compute, data, industry scenarios, and research fields. It is expressive and strategic, but must avoid becoming a generic network diagram in final execution.

### 2. Color system

**A — Institutional Dark**  
Deep navy-led, authoritative, suitable for keynote visuals, official announcements, reports, stage screens, and government / enterprise-facing materials. It gives the brand a research-infrastructure feel rather than an education-app feel.

**B — Research Light**  
White and cloud-gray-led, clearer for admissions pages, dashboards, databases, training interfaces, certificates, and everyday institutional communication. It keeps the system open and accessible for students while preserving technical clarity.

**C — Recruitment Energy**  
Keeps the navy / blue base but permits small orange interventions for open days, bootcamps, countdowns, and calls to action. The orange should remain a tactical signal, not a primary brand color, to avoid knowledge-commerce cues.

### 3. Typography and system language

**A — Compact institutional sans**  
Most formal and stable. Works for official identity, signage, certificates, reports, and partner-facing decks. Chinese remains primary, with English secondary and disciplined.

**B — Variable-width research OS**  
Most conceptually aligned with “paths expanding” and “research directions unfolding.” It can become the signature system language across web, motion, and program architecture if implemented with a variable type and grid rules.

**C — Controlled high-energy youth system**  
Most recruitment-friendly. It introduces speed and activation while still constrained by the grid. Best as a campaign layer, not necessarily the master institutional voice.

## Recommended synthesis for next stage

Use **Logo B** as the master generative logic, borrowing the Chinese-name specificity of **Logo A** for the primary lockup. Pair **Color A** for flagship / institutional surfaces with **Color B** for digital product and information interfaces. Use **Typography B** as the core system language, with **Typography A** for formal documents and **Typography C** reserved for recruitment or event campaigns.
