## Strengths

- Corrects the most important naming issue: **上海创智学院** is now consistently used.
- Avoids AI-design clichés: no blue-purple trust gradient, no emoji icons, no fake metrics, no dense hallucination-prone copy.
- The system board demonstrates credible extension into lockups, tokens, graphic language, and application previews.
- Poster is appropriately minimal and high-status rather than commercial or training-brand-like.

## Minor Recommendations Before Production

1. **Unify the mark.** Choose whether the official symbol is the standalone open-evidence mark, the square diagonal lockup icon, or the dimensional portal mark shown on the board; do not ship all three as equal primaries.
2. **Create text-safe templates.** Keep generated poster copy limited to approved locked text fields, especially for Chinese admissions materials.
3. **Codify accent rules.** Preserve cyan as the primary signal color; reserve lime/orange only for campaign-level variants.

## Final Call

**SHIP** for concept presentation and brand direction approval; **minor iteration** needed only for production-grade logo consistency.
