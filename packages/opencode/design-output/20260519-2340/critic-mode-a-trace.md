## Rewrite Suggestions

### Color Strategy Rewrite
Use Deep Institute Navy `#071526`, Warm White `#F7F4EC`, Graphite `#101114`, and Lab Silver `#D8E1E8` for 80–90% of surfaces. Use Cognitive Cyan `#00D1C7` as the only primary brand accent for links, nodes, active states, and key institutional signals. Reserve either Signal Lime `#B7FF2A` or Breakthrough Orange `#FF6B2C` as a single campaign-level secondary accent; never use both in the same core identity asset. Violet-blue `#6657FF` may appear only as a narrow research-direction code for AI theory/science at <3% of an asset and must not become a gradient endpoint or dominant color.

### Symbol & Mark Rewrite
Primary mark concept: abstract 创/智 into one open gateway and evidence-window symbol. A diagonal boundary-cut derived from 创 intersects a rectangular observation window derived from 智, with three precise nodes representing student, science, and industry. The mark must work in one color, at favicon scale, and as a corner anchor on research documents. Do not combine the logo with literal chips, brains, shields, globes, robots, or city landmarks. Use reasoning chains, coordinate fields, and Shanghai West Bund grid traces only as secondary graphic-system elements.

### Typography Rewrite
Use a hard, slightly condensed Chinese grotesk for display headings, paired with IBM Plex Sans or a Suisse-style rational/humanist grotesk for English and body copy. Use IBM Plex Mono only for codes, coordinates, dates, version labels, and research parameters. Display headings should feel tight and architectural; uppercase English labels should be letter-spaced; body copy should remain calm and highly readable. Do not use a single neutral sans style for every text layer.

### Visual Direction Refinement
Prioritize the “research task interface” over decorative AI abstraction. Each generated asset should include at least one of the following concrete mechanisms: an evidence window, a weighted reasoning path, a coordinate field, a task-node map, or a coded research-direction label. Remove any graphic element that cannot be explained as student-centered support, frontier exploration, research-to-engineering validation, open ecosystem, or deployment/value transfer.
