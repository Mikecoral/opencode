# Asset Plan: 创智学院 / Shanghai Innovation Institute
Design goal: Build a credible, high-density identity extension for a new AI research-and-talent institution: “an open research-creation operating system” that can carry admissions, research release, events, web, and campus-space needs without falling into generic AI-tech or traditional university visuals.

Research note: The official SII site confirms the bilingual name, active sections for admissions, research, industry cooperation, recruitment, and the public tagline “集不凡·创奇迹 | Calling for Marvellers,” with current content spanning AI Infra, robotics, large-model reasoning, scientific intelligence, and recruitment.
