# Asset Generation Plan

## Rationale
上海创智学院 / Shanghai Innovation Institute is positioned as a hybrid of national-level AI research institute, graduate talent community, and industry innovation ecosystem. The brief emphasizes modular, dynamic, bilingual, non-traditional academic branding that must work across recruitment camps, research releases, forums, campus space, and digital media. These assets therefore prioritize a scalable identity core plus high-value touchpoints from Section 10: admissions, research publication, academic events, digital UI, campus wayfinding, and community identity.
