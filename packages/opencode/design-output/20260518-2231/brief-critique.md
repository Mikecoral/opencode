- PASS — brief is strong, proceed to design

- Total cited sources: 8
- Authoritative sources (official site, government, major media/recognized institutions): 8
- Unverified or weak: None significant. The IBM source is not organization-specific, but is acceptable as a broad AI-visual-language reference rather than a factual source about the institute.

## Required Fixes (if REVISE or RESEARCH-AGAIN)
None required. Optional improvements before design generation:
1. Section 7 Color Strategy — add approximate HEX ranges for deep blue-black, electric cyan, warm orange/red, silver gray, and mist white.
2. Section 8 Typography Direction — add candidate Chinese and Latin typefaces or style references, especially for bilingual academic/recruitment use.

## Strengths
The brief is well sourced, strategically specific, and highly executable. It distinguishes Shanghai Innovation Institute from generic education branding by framing it as a national-strategy AI talent ecosystem with research, industry, entrepreneurship, and youth-energy dimensions. The application list is unusually concrete and should give designers enough context to build a coherent, multi-touchpoint identity system.
