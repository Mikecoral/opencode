# Designer Trace

## Phase 1: Asset Planning

### Subject Type & Brief Touchpoints
Subject Type from brief: “organization.”

Relevant Section 11 touchpoints classified for this run:
- Top priority — 科研成果 / 项目展示模板: “学院持续发布高技术密度成果，需要把复杂项目转化为可信、可传播的视觉信息。”
- Top priority — 招生营 / 博士遴选信息系统: “招生遴选是当前明确、高频、关键的外部触点，需要统一考试大纲、时间安排、机考手册、营会 KV。”
- Top priority — Event key visual：TechFest / 开放日 / 成果发布会: “开放活动是公众认知学院‘新型学校’的窗口，适合建立高识别度主视觉。”
- Top priority — 官网与社交内容系统: “官网承担招生、科研、人事、媒体传播多重任务，需统一栏目视觉、新闻卡片和英文/中文切换体验。”
- Optional — 人才招聘 / 青年教师引进视觉: “适合在主 VI 稳定后扩展招聘子系统。”
- Optional — Sticker / pin / hoodie / tote 社区周边: “适合学生项目、开放日、黑客松传播。”
- Not this round — 校园导视 / 大楼门头: “第一轮更应优先解决科研传播、招生和事件识别；导视可待主标和网格系统稳定后展开。”

### Visual Direction Decision
- Selected direction: research-signal
- Alternatives considered: institutional-minimal, campaign-energy, community-culture
- Why selected: Section 5 names “AI Infra / 算力 / 网格,” “前沿信号 / 动态雷达,” and “破界与跨学科”; Section 7 includes “前沿信号、算力网格、动态拓扑、科学可信.” This is stronger evidence for a signal/grid research system than for a purely institutional or campaign-led direction.
- Generic defaults avoided: traditional education admissions bundle, campus/environmental bundle, generic AI gradient hero bundle, merchandise-first maker-community bundle.

### Deliverable Strategy Decision
- Selected strategy: digital-system
- Why selected: Section 11 prioritizes official website/social content, research/project display, admissions information, and event key visuals. These are recurring digital communication surfaces that need shared structure and visual rules.
- Why other strategies were rejected or deferred: identity-core alone would under-serve the Top-priority content templates; campaign-system alone would overemphasize TechFest/open day at the expense of ongoing research updates; spatial-system is explicitly Not this round; community-system and recruiting visuals are Optional and should follow after the core digital language stabilizes.

### Why this final 4-8?
The final seven assets form one research-signal digital system: a grid-generated identity anchor, a modular glyph logic, and four recurring digital communication surfaces for research, website/social, events, and admissions information. Omitted defaults such as color palette, campus signage, brochures, and merchandise were not generated because Section 11 either deprioritizes them or frames them as future extensions rather than this round’s strategic need.
