## Strengths
The revised brief is strategically stronger than the earlier “AI 无人区” framing because it preserves exploration and frontier ambition without reducing the institution to a single slogan. The cultural creative direction is not generic school merchandise: it is tied to the main identity system through variable grids, research-field badges, code/project labels, identity cards, and restrained wearable graphics. The application map is evidence-based and avoids default “school brochure/campus merch” thinking by prioritizing research publishing, training camps, talks, recruitment, and a justified community product system.
