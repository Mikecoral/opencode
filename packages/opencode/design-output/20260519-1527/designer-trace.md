# Designer Trace

## Phase 1: Asset Planning

### Subject Type & Brief Touchpoints
Subject Type from brief: “organization.”

Relevant Section 11 classifications:
- Top priority: 科研成果发布模板（官网卡片、长图、论文/开源项目封面） — “官网‘科研进展’已有 67 条记录…最需要统一科研信息层级.”
- Top priority: 招生营 / 训练营视觉系统 — “学院以招生营、创客大赛、训练营进行项目制遴选.”
- Top priority: 创智讲坛 / 学术报告海报模板 — “官网首页‘创智日历’与学术报告信息突出.”
- Top priority: 人才引进与青年教师招募 campaign — “全球引进青年教师 / Calling for Marvellers.”
- Top priority: 文创与社群纪念品系统 — “用户明确要求加入文创相关设计.”
- Optional: 高校/企业合作 lockup 规范 — frequent, but brief says main visual system should come first.
- Optional: 空间导视与玻璃幕墙标识 — important later because of independent building and glass curtain wall context.
- Not this round: 传统纸质招生册 — brief says the stronger evidence is project-based digital communication.

### Visual Direction Decision
- Selected direction: `research-signal`
- Alternatives considered: `institutional-minimal`, `campaign-energy`, `community-culture`
- Why selected: Section 5 names “前沿信号 / 网格系统,” “开放代码 / 开源科研,” and “具身与科学智能”; Section 10 defines mark routes based on dynamic grids, signal waves, and spark-node structures.
- Generic defaults avoided: education admissions bundle, campus signage bundle, traditional school crest, slogan-led recruitment campaign, generic tech neon board.

### Deliverable Strategy Decision
- Selected strategy: `identity-core`
- Why selected: User requested multiple logo designs with differentiated styles; Section 10 already provides symbol/mark strategy evidence; Section 12 requires the Signal Kit to come from the main brand system rather than a separate merchandise style.
- Why other strategies were rejected or deferred: `campaign-system` is tempting because admissions, lectures, and recruiting are Top priority, but it would split the output across several communication systems before the logo is resolved. `spatial-system` is Optional. `community-system` is partially addressed through one Signal Kit NFC card, but not selected as primary because the identity exploration is the core request.

### Why this final 4-8?
The final six assets satisfy the user’s multi-logo exploration request with four distinct logo directions, then add one high-priority research communication template and one focused cultural creative Signal Kit object. Omitted defaults such as color-palette, campus signage, admissions poster, brochure, and generic merchandise were not generated because they would dilute the selected identity-core strategy or are explicitly Optional/Not this round in the brief.
