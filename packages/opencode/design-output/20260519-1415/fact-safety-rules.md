# Fact-Safety Rules for 创智学院 Brand Assets

This iteration must reduce hallucination and avoid invented factual content.

## Allowed Facts
- Organization names: 上海创智学院, Shanghai Innovation Institute, SII.
- Positioning: AI frontier, project-based talent cultivation, student-centered learning, mentor-group cultivation, industry-research collaboration.
- Verified touchpoints from the brief:招生培养, 科研创新, 人事人才, 创智讲坛, 招生营/夏令营, 青年教师招聘, 科研进展, PPT/报告传播.
- Verified context from the brief: Shanghai Xuhui West Bund / 北杨人工智能小镇, compute resources, AI infrastructure, mentor network, coding/math assessment context.
- Verified recruiting phrase from the official homepage: Calling for Marvellers.

## Disallowed Content
- No invented speaker names, event dates, venues, paper titles, awards, research results, model names, rankings, enrollment numbers, funding numbers, slogans, or statistics.
- No pseudo-readable microtext that looks like real data.
- No fabricated QR codes, URLs, departments, lab names, or partner names.
- No fake institutional seals, certifications, or official-looking approvals.
- No UI panels that imply real dashboards, performance metrics, or unreleased projects.

## Placeholder Policy
- If a template requires mutable content, use explicit placeholders such as `[Speaker Name]`, `[Date]`, `[Venue]`, `[Research Topic]`, `[QR Placeholder]`.
- Placeholders must look like editable template fields, not real facts.

## Visual Policy
- Prioritize clean identity, hierarchy, and reusable templates over decorative AI-dashboard detail.
- Use fewer nodes, fewer labels, and more whitespace.
- Keep diagrams abstract unless the label is verified or an explicit placeholder.
