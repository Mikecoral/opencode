## Retest Pattern

- Younger profiles improve the perceived memorability of `logo-primary`, `summer-camp-mission-card`, and `ppt-report-cover-system`.
- Accessibility remains the lowest dimension across research, summer camp, recruitment, and PPT assets.
- `faculty-recruitment-banner` remains the weakest on specificity because it does not clearly answer "what role, what benefit, how to apply".
- `chuangzhi-forum-poster` is the most improved asset in retest; its structured event hierarchy is easier for a mixed sample to understand than expected.

## Robust Conclusions Across Both Tests

1. The visual system is credible and memorable.
2. The system is still too generic to "advanced AI institution" unless SII/Shanghai/bilingual identity is made more explicit.
3. Broader-audience accessibility needs simplified language, fewer decorative panels, and larger essential text.
4. Recruitment and summer camp assets need direct calls to action and concrete eligibility/application information.
