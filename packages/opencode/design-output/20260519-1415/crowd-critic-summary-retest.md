# Crowd Critic Summary Retest

## High-Level Verdict

**Retest confirms the first crowd result, with a slightly higher score due to more young applicant-adjacent profiles.** The system remains visually credible, premium, and memorable. The main risk is still not visual quality; it is whether different audiences can quickly understand what SII uniquely offers and what action they should take.

**Estimated retest crowd score:** 7.3/10.

## Top Repeated Audience Concerns

1. **SII specificity is still under-authored.** The system signals advanced AI, but could belong to many AI labs unless the lockups, bilingual hierarchy, Shanghai/West Bund context, and real SII facts are added.
2. **Applicant and recruitment pathways are too implicit.** Young profiles like the style but ask: who is this for, what do I get, how do I apply, what happens next?
3. **Accessibility remains the weak dimension.** Dense technical language, small labels, and abstract diagrams reduce public/older/small-city comprehension.

## Robust Designer Actions

1. Keep the research-signal direction; it is working.
2. Add controlled bilingual SII identity and Shanghai/West Bund cues across all public-facing assets.
3. Reduce microtext and decorative dashboard panels by about one third.
4. For `summer-camp-mission-card.png` and `faculty-recruitment-banner.png`, add clear `who / why / how / when` modules.
5. For public or partner decks, add one plain-language impact line so the system does not feel like style-only AI infrastructure.
