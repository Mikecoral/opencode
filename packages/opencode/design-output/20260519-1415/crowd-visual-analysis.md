## Pattern Summary

- Technical and highly educated profiles rate the system as credible, ambitious, and aligned with an AI institute.
- Older, lower-education, or broader-public profiles find the visuals impressive but hard to decode.
- The strongest shared concern is not aesthetics; it is specificity and accessibility.
