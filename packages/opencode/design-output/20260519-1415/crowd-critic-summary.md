# Crowd Critic Summary: Basic Evaluation

## High-Level Verdict

**Positive but accessibility-limited.** The crowd simulation broadly reads the system as modern, credible, and appropriate for a serious AI institute. It performs best for technical, highly educated, urban, academic, and industry-adjacent viewers. It performs less well for older, less technical, or public-facing audiences because the visual language is dense, abstract, English-heavy, and sometimes generic to AI institutions.

**Estimated crowd score:** 7.1/10.

## Top Repeated Audience Concerns

1. **Specificity gap:** The assets say "advanced AI institute" clearly, but do not yet say "Shanghai Innovation Institute / 上海创智学院" uniquely enough. The logo, forum poster, and report covers are most exposed to this risk.
2. **Accessibility and legibility:** Dense microtext, technical labels, and AI-dashboard panels reduce comprehension for older, public, cross-disciplinary, and non-specialist audiences.
3. **Concrete proof and action missing:** Recruitment and summer camp assets need clearer CTAs, eligibility/application paths, named resources, faculty/mentor proof, project examples, and institutional credibility signals.
