# Design Direction Options — 创智学院

## Direction 1: 智慧中枢 / Intelligence Grid

**Strategic rationale:** Fits 创智学院 as a serious AI-and-innovation academy that wants to signal structured learning, systems thinking and industry relevance.  
**Visual register:** Institutional authority + precise engineering + modular intelligence.  
**Color logic:** Deep intelligence indigo `#0B1B5E` + electric cyan `#17D6E8` + clean white `#FFFFFF` + graphite `#1E2430`; trust foundation with a controlled AI-energy accent.  
**Typography logic:** Neo-grotesk Chinese/Latin sans with tight, engineered spacing; optional monospaced numerals/code accents for course levels, labs and data points.  
**Mark concept:** A modular grid mark where “创 / 智 / C / Z” can be abstracted into connected nodes or learning pathways; expandable into course glyphs.  
**Key reference:** MIT Media Lab identity by Pentagram — seven-by-seven grid generates master mark and research-group glyphs.  
**What to avoid:** Do not become a generic circuit-board / brain-network cliché with too many glowing lines.

## Direction 2: 人本启智 / Human Spark

**Strategic rationale:** Fits a student- and professional-learner academy that wants AI to feel empowering, optimistic and accessible rather than cold or elite.  
**Visual register:** Warm edtech + future optimism + approachable intelligence.  
**Color logic:** Royal learning blue `#1226AA` + sunrise orange `#FF8A00` + warm cream `#FFF7E6` + growth green `#35B779`; technology anchored by human energy and growth.  
**Typography logic:** Rounded humanist sans for Chinese and Latin; open counters, generous line height, friendly numerals; softer icons and illustration compatibility.  
**Mark concept:** A spark/seed/book hybrid that suggests “启智” — knowledge igniting capability; can animate as a learning path opening from one point.  
**Key reference:** Squirrel AI Learning — royal-blue identity combined with approachable student imagery and learning-path illustration.  
**What to avoid:** Do not become childish K–12 tutoring; the warmth must still work for young professionals and industry learners.

## Direction 3: 新书院 / Future Academy Seal

**Strategic rationale:** Fits 创智学院 if it wants to own a more premium Chinese academy identity: scholarly, future-facing and culturally grounded.  
**Visual register:** Contemporary scholastic authority + cultural depth + intelligent transformation.  
**Color logic:** Ink navy `#0A1628` + jade/teal `#00A887` + muted scholar gold `#CFAE4A` + porcelain white `#F8F6F0`; combines Chinese academic gravitas with modern innovation.  
**Typography logic:** Modern Songti/serif accent for the Chinese name paired with a restrained sans for information; contrast between “academy heritage” and “technology clarity.”  
**Mark concept:** A contemporary seal/window mark combining the structure of “创” and “智” with an open book or portal; simplified enough for app icons and certificates.  
**Key reference:** HKUST Brand Assets Unit / university identity system — emblematic authority, official colors/fonts and centralized brand governance.  
**What to avoid:** Do not imitate traditional seals literally or become government/institutional-heavy; keep the form clean, open and digital-ready.
