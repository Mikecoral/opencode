## Step 4 — Dimension Scoring Reasoning
### Strategy Alignment — 8.5/10
The brief asks for a future-facing but academically credible “智慧中枢 / Intelligence Grid” system built from modular grid logic rather than AI clichés. The assets deliver that direction consistently through 7×7 construction, pathway nodes, deep indigo trust, cyan intelligence signals, and applications across platform/certificate/campaign contexts. Minor concern: the tech-grid visual language may still read more “AI platform” than “academy” unless the certificate/formal typography layer is expanded.

### Logo Quality — 7.5/10
The mark is scalable, clear, and consistent with the C/Z/path brief. It works at small size and has a practical app-icon silhouette. However, C/Z monograms with node paths are common in tech education, so distinctiveness depends on further custom geometry. The lockup’s mark variation also shows the AI generation interpreting the symbol slightly differently, indicating the need for vector standardization.

### Color System — 8.0/10
The palette is intentional: deep indigo builds institutional trust, cyan carries active intelligence, white supports order, and gold/amber are restrained. It avoids the generic AI gradient trap. The only craft weakness is cyan frequency in node-heavy compositions; the system needs active/inactive rules so cyan does not become overused.

### Typography — 8.0/10
The typographic direction follows the brief: modern Hei-style Chinese, rational English caps, monospaced technical labels. The English tracking is visibly appropriate. Generated Chinese glyphs are credible but slightly over-engineered/blocky; final production should choose real Chinese/Latin typefaces and redraw logo text rather than relying on imagegen forms.

### Application Coherence — 8.2/10
The system carries across six assets with shared grid, color, typography, and pathway logic. It demonstrates a usable identity system, not just a logo. The platform is the least resolved application because its dense matrix of codes creates usability concerns; the certificate’s seal icon also introduces a slightly generic academic symbol that could be made more ownable.
