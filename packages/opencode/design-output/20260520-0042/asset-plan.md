# Asset Plan: 创智学院
Design goal: Build a focused “Intelligence Grid” identity system for a future-facing but credible innovation/AI academy: structured, technical, academic, and extensible across digital learning, certification, and recruitment/campaign communication.

## Focused primary mark direction

Use one mark route only: a 7×7 modular grid generating a CZ monogram. The C is an open geometric outer frame; the Z is a three-segment learning path moving from lower-left origin to upper-right outcome, connecting exactly 5 visible nodes. Total nodes must not exceed 7, line weight is uniform, corners are crisp or very slightly softened, and the mark must remain legible at favicon/app-icon scale. Avoid brains, robots, circuit boards, lightbulbs, rockets, shields, laurel wreaths, and generic dense node-network graphics.

## Color palette and usage limits

- Deep Intelligence Indigo `#0B1B5E`: primary institutional color; 55–70% of dark-surface assets; use for mark, headings, deep backgrounds, and formal identity fields.
- Cool White `#F6F8FB`: primary light background; 20–35% of most layouts; creates academic clarity and calm spacing.
- Pure White `#FFFFFF`: only for reverse type, mark knockout, or controlled negative space; avoid large sterile fields when `#F6F8FB` is sufficient.
- Graphite Black `#1E2430`: text and interface hierarchy; 10–20% of layouts; use for body copy, labels, and secondary dark surfaces.
- Intelligent Cyan `#17D6E8`: single AI signal accent; max 8% of any asset; use only on nodes, path endpoints, CTA highlights, micro-glyphs, and data/path indicators.
- Academic Gold `#C6A45D`: achievement/formal accent; max 3%; use only for certificate seals, achievement badges, or a final pathway endpoint.
- Warm Amber `#D9903D`: optional replacement for gold in youth/campaign contexts; max 3%; never use gold and amber together in the same asset.

## Chinese / English typography guidance

- Chinese display: open, engineered modern Hei style referencing Source Han Sans SC / Alibaba PuHuiTi 3.0 / Sarasa Gothic; 600–700 weight; tight but readable tracking; strong grid alignment.
- Chinese body: same family category at 400–500 weight; generous line spacing; avoid calligraphic, seal-script, overly rounded, or children’s education styles.
- English display and UI: rational neo-grotesk or geometric sans referencing IBM Plex Sans, Söhne, Akkurat, or Space Grotesk; 600–700 for headings, 400–500 for interface/body.
- Codes, labels, and pathway numbers: monospaced or semi-mono referencing IBM Plex Mono / JetBrains Mono; all-caps English labels use +0.08em tracking; course codes use consistent `AI LAB 01`, `PATHWAY A`, `LEVEL 02` style.
- Do not render the entire system in one uniform default sans; hierarchy must show weight, spacing, and mono-code contrast.
