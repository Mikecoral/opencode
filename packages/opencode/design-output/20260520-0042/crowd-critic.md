2. **Accent repetition**: Cyan nodes dominate pathway assets beyond brief intent. (Observed in 15/24 profiles, especially young professionals.)

## Conflicting Feedback

- Rhythm rigidity: Older audiences endorse predictability; younger audiences advocate for dynamic shifts.
