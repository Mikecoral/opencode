# Brand Design Brief: 上海创智学院 / Shanghai Innovation Institute (SII)

> Prepared by the Brand Strategist agent · 2026-05-19
> Source of record: official site `sii.edu.cn`, Baidu Baike, The Paper (澎湃), Jiemian (界面), 人民日报大江东, Sohu, Zhihu reports, LinkedIn (sii2024).

---

## 1. Organization Overview

**上海创智学院 / Shanghai Innovation Institute (SII)** is a brand-new type of Chinese higher-education + research institution, established **July 2024**. It is *not* a traditional university — it has no undergraduate program, no department walls, and no fixed degree timeline. It is closer in spirit to a **graduate-level AI moonshot academy** modelled loosely on MIT Media Lab, DeepMind's research culture, and Y Combinator's founder ethos — but rooted in Shanghai and embedded in China's national AI strategy.

**Key facts**
- **Address:** 上海市徐汇区华发路699弄3号 — inside the **北杨人工智能小镇 (Beiyang AI Town)** on Xuhui West Bund (徐汇滨江), facing the Huangpu River. New 27,000 sqm purpose-built independent building.
- **Founded by a consortium** of **31 top universities and research institutes + 20+ leading enterprises** (Shanghai Jiao Tong, Fudan, Tongji, Shanghai AI Lab, etc., partnering with industry).
- **Leadership:** 党委书记/常务副院长 Ding Xiaodong (丁晓东). Star faculty include 90后 mentor Liu Pengfei (刘鹏飞, Generative AI Research Lab).
- **Scale (as of year 1):** ~600 students + faculty, 81 full-time mentors, plus part-time academic mentors and industry mentors. Target compute: a **10,000-GPU (万卡)** cluster.
- **Programs:** PhD-track selection in AI Infrastructure, AI-for-Science, Large Models, Embodied Intelligence / Robotics, Multimodal (MOSS-VL), AI-Bio (broad-spectrum antibodies, 1-micron biohybrid robots).
- **Brand artifacts already in market:** product line "**创奇·智能新引擎 / ChuangQi — Marvellous Intelligence New Engine**"; lecture series "**创智讲坛**"; recruiting campaign "**集不凡·创奇迹 | Calling for Marvellers**"; annual "**TechFest 创智未来大会**"; "**金秋营**" (Golden Autumn Camp) selection event.
- **Mission statement (paraphrased from site):**  
  *"以学生为中心、以前沿为牵引"* — cultivate **China's AI 领军人才 (leadership-grade talent)** — specifically a new species the institute calls **"Researcher Founder / 研究型创业者"**: scientist + entrepreneur in one person, who can "**一顶一、掰手腕**" (go one-on-one, arm-wrestle) with the world's best.
- **Pedagogical pillars (the "Three Supers"):** 超高规格的师资 · 超常措施的培养 · 超凡条件的保障 (ultra-high-spec faculty, unconventional cultivation, extraordinary support).
- **Academic framework:** 一体两翼 ("one body, two wings") — Chuangzhi Forum as the spine, mentor-series + student-series as wings.

This is a **state-strategic, ambitious, well-resourced, intentionally non-traditional** institution. Its brand must signal: *not another university logo*.

## 2. Brand Positioning

**Positioning statement (proposed):**  
> *上海创智学院 — 中国 AI 领军人才的"奇境"。  
> The place in Shanghai where China's most extraordinary minds become both scientists and founders — and arm-wrestle the future.*

**Strategic position on the map:**

|  | Tradition / Authority | Frontier / Risk-taking |
|---|---|---|
| **Academic** | Tsinghua AIR, Peking U AI, Fudan | **(Westlake Univ.)** |
| **Founder / Builder** | (Industry labs) | **🟧 SII sits HERE** — and uniquely fuses lab rigor with founder velocity |

**Differentiation vs. peers:**

- **MIT Media Lab** — playful, anti-disciplinary, eccentric polychrome identity (Pentagram, 2014). SII shares the *anti-departmental* DNA but is more **mission-serious** and more **bilingual/Chinese-rooted**.
- **Mila (Montréal)** — clean academic blue, soft humanism. Too quiet for SII's ambition.
- **Tsinghua AIR (清华智能产业研究院)** — corporate-academic, conservative red/grey. SII must look *younger, more daring*.
- **Shanghai AI Lab (上海人工智能实验室)** — purple/blue, technical, government-adjacent restraint. SII is a **sibling institution** but is the *talent academy*, not the lab — must feel **more human, more founder-energy, less infrastructure**.
- **Westlake University** — elegant grey-blue, classical Western academic. SII should reject the "instant heritage" move and instead **claim newness**.
- **DeepMind / OpenAI** — Western tech-brand polish. SII should borrow *confidence*, not visual language; it must read as **distinctly Chinese-modern**, not derivative.

**Ownable territory:** The intersection of **frontier-research seriousness** and **founder-velocity playfulness**, expressed through **a Shanghai-Bund modernist aesthetic** with **Chinese ideographic intelligence**. No peer owns this.

**One-line essence:** *"奇境 / Wonderland for arm-wrestling the future."* — combining 创 (create), 智 (intelligence), 奇 (marvellous/extraordinary).

## 3. Target Audience

**Primary (must love the brand):**

1. **Prospective PhD candidates, age 21–28** — the top 1% of CS/EE/Math/Bio graduates from Tsinghua, PKU, SJTU, Fudan, USTC, ZJU, plus returnees from CMU/MIT/Stanford. They are choosing between SII, Tsinghua AIR, Westlake, Shanghai AI Lab, or a US PhD. They are **builders, gamers, hackers, paper-readers**; they respond to **substance + edge**, not pomp.
2. **Existing 600+ students and faculty** — the brand must make them feel they belong to something *historic and slightly cult-like*. Internal merch, lanyards, hoodies, lab stickers matter as much as the official logo.
3. **Star faculty being recruited globally** — "集不凡·创奇迹 | Calling for Marvellers". They are evaluating SII against Stanford, MIT, DeepMind, Tsinghua. Brand must convey *gravitas + freedom*.

**Secondary:**

4. **Industry partners & enterprise mentors** — Huawei, Alibaba, Tencent, ByteDance, SenseTime, MiniMax, Moonshot, etc. The brand must look **peer-credible at boardroom level** without becoming corporate.
5. **Government & Shanghai municipal stakeholders** — the brand must register as a **landmark of Shanghai's AI ambition**, on par with West Bund as a cultural-economic zone.
6. **International academic community** — visiting Turing laureates (Jack Dongarra has lectured there), conference partners, foreign students. English bilingual presence is **non-negotiable**.
7. **Press & general public** — when CCTV or 澎湃新闻 photograph the building, the wordmark on the glass façade must read as iconic.

**Psychographic core:** *high-agency, intellectually restless, allergic to bureaucratic aesthetics, fluent in both Chinese tradition and global tech culture.*

## 4. Brand Personality & Values

**Core values (distilled from the institute's own language):**

1. **前沿 (Frontier)** — "以前沿为牵引" — always pointing at the unknown edge.
2. **创造 (Create)** — 创奇迹, 创新, 创业 — making, not curating.
3. **领军 (Leadership)** — not following, not catching up — *leading*.
4. **不凡 (Marvellous / Extraordinary)** — "集不凡·创奇迹" is the recruitment war cry.
5. **掰手腕 (Arm-wrestle)** — bold, competitive, unembarrassed about ambition.
6. **以学生为中心 (Student-centric)** — human warmth, not institutional cold.
7. **开放协同 (Open collaboration)** — 31 universities × 20+ enterprises × global mentors.

**Personality (as a person):** A 28-year-old PhD-founder who reads Borges and trains transformers, wears black, drinks oolong, has a Shanghai postcode and a GitHub graph that never sleeps. Confident, witty, allergic to clichés, generous with knowledge, slightly mischievous, intensely serious about the work.

**Tone of voice:**
- **Chinese:** 简练、有力、带锋芒，不端着，不官话。Mix of 古典凝练 (古文式短句) and 当代锐利 (verb-driven). Examples: "**集不凡，创奇迹**" "**一顶一，掰手腕**" "**让代码改变世界**".
- **English:** Crisp, declarative, lightly literary. Avoid "innovation ecosystem synergy" corporate-speak. Examples: *"Calling for Marvellers."* *"Build the unbuilt."* *"From paper to product, from lab to launch."*

**Anti-personality (what we are NOT):**
- ❌ A traditional university (no Latin mottos, no ribbon-scrolls, no oak leaves).
- ❌ A government bureau (no red-gold seal aesthetic, no Song-typeface gravitas).
- ❌ A startup (no rainbow-gradient SaaS logo, no "AI" lowercase wordmark in Inter).
- ❌ A Western lab pastiche (no DeepMind teal, no OpenAI hex).

## 5. Design Keywords

Pick these as the conceptual generators for every visual decision. Six anchor keywords + four amplifiers.

**Anchor (must be visibly present):**

1. **奇境 / Wonderland** — the institute is repeatedly described as 奇境 ("a wondrous place"). Captures both 创**奇** and 创**智**'s playfulness. Visual cue: spatial depth, an *interior to enter*.
2. **掰手腕 / Arm-wrestle** — competitive tension, two forces locked. Visual cue: opposing geometry, balance under load, the *moment before release*.
3. **西岸光 / West Bund Light** — Huangpu river reflections, glass curtain walls of Beiyang AI Town, dawn-over-Pudong palette. *Specifically Shanghai, specifically now.*
4. **算 / Compute** — the 10,000-GPU 万卡 cluster as a civic monument. Grids, lattices, dense matrices made beautiful.
5. **手稿 / Manuscript** — frontier research lives on whiteboards (Liu Pengfei's "白板上密密麻麻"). The brand should feel hand-thought, not template-generated.
6. **创·智·奇 (三字魂)** — the three characters that make the institute's name and ethos. The mark should somehow let these three glyphs *do work*.

**Amplifiers (atmosphere):**

7. **当代水墨 / Contemporary ink** — restrained, modern reinterpretation of Chinese ink — *not* dragon-cloud kitsch.
8. **少年气 / Youthful edge** — the 90后 / 95后 mentor culture; sharp, slightly cocky, alive.
9. **精密 / Precision** — Swiss-grid discipline as a counterweight to playfulness.
10. **开源 / Open** — open-source ethic; brand assets should feel *forkable*.

## 6. Visual Direction

**The big idea — one ownable concept:**  
> **"奇境之门 / The Gate to Wonderland"** — SII's identity is built around a **portal/threshold motif**: a geometric opening that frames intelligence as a space to *enter*, not a logo to *look at*. Inside the portal lives a typographic + glyphic system that breathes. The portal can be square, arched, or layered — it is the consistent gesture across the system, the way Penguin owns the oval or MIT Media Lab owns the dot-array.

**Why a portal:**
- 学**院** literally contains 门 (gate) in the radical of 院.
- 创智 in Chinese culture echoes 桃花源 (the Wonderland), 龙门 (the dragon-gate), 月洞门 (Suzhou-garden moon gate) — the Chinese visual archive is *full* of doorways-into-other-worlds.
- The Beiyang campus building itself is a portal: a glass cube facing the river.
- Functionally, a portal is **a frame** — it can hold faculty portraits, research figures, lecture posters, recruiting copy, AI-generated imagery. It's a system, not a stamp.

**Mood references (composite — describe to the Designer agent):**

- **Pentagram × MIT Media Lab (2014, Michael Bierut + E Roon Kang)** — for the *system-over-stamp* logic and confidence with negative space; but warmer, less algorithmic.
- **Kenya Hara / MUJI** — for restraint, white space, the feeling that the brand is the **container**, not the noise.
- **Wang Xu / 王序 + Han Jiaying** — Chinese modernist graphic design; ink discipline + Swiss grid.
- **He Jianping (何见平) posters** — sharp typographic + ideographic interplay, Chinese characters as visual material.
- **West Bund Museum & Long Museum (Atelier Deshaus)** — raw concrete + light + river; SII's physical neighborhood is its mood board.
- **Stripe Press / Are.na** — the editorial confidence; serious-but-not-stuffy.
- **Shanghai Biennale catalogues** — high-end Chinese contemporary art design language.
- **NOT references:** generic AI-circuit-board mandalas, blue-gradient brain illustrations, neural-network glow, Inter wordmarks on dark backgrounds.

**Visual metaphors to deploy:**
- A **doorway/portal** (the consistent shape).
- A **whiteboard mid-thought** — equations, sketches, arrows allowed.
- A **lattice of compute nodes** — but rendered as ink dots, not Tron lines.
- A **Bund skyline at dawn** — horizon line as a recurring rule across layouts.
- An **open book / open hand** — 学 + 院, learning + welcome.

## 7. Color Strategy

**Strategic rationale:** Reject the AI-industry blue default. Anchor the palette in **Shanghai's actual light** — the cool stone-grey of West Bund concrete, the warm ochre of evening river-light on glass, and a single decisive **signature accent** that becomes "SII orange" the way IBM owns blue.

**Proposed palette:**

| Role | Name | Hex | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Primary signature** | **创智朱 / Chuangzhi Vermilion** | `#E94B1F` | A modern vermilion — pulled from Chinese 朱砂 (cinnabar / vermilion ink seals) and pushed warmer toward signal-orange. Reads as *bold, Chinese, unmistakable*. This is the "掰手腕" color — competitive, alive. Used sparingly and decisively. |
| **Primary ground** | **西岸墨 / West Bund Ink** | `#0E1116` | Near-black with a hint of blue-grey. The color of a powered-on monitor at 2am, and of wet Shanghai pavement. Default for serious surfaces. |
| **Secondary** | **江雾白 / River-Mist White** | `#F6F4EF` | Off-white with warm undertone. Replaces sterile `#FFFFFF`. Carries paper / manuscript / contemporary-ink quality. |
| **Secondary** | **算力灰 / Compute Grey** | `#9AA0A6` | Cool neutral for grids, captions, secondary type. |
| **Accent** | **晨光金 / Dawn Gold** | `#D9A441` | Used very sparingly for ceremonial moments (diplomas, anniversary marks). Bund-at-dawn warmth. |
| **Accent** | **奇境青 / Wonderland Cyan** | `#1F6F8B` | A deep, slightly inky teal — for data-vis, hyperlinks, and the *frontier* moments. NOT a brand-blue; it must always sit beside vermilion, never replace it. |

**Pairing rule:** 80% ink + mist (calm canvas) · 15% grey + cyan (structure) · **5% vermilion (the punch)**. The vermilion does the work. Everything else holds the room.

**What we explicitly avoid:** AI-lab blue (#2D5BFF family), purple gradients, neon green, rainbow brand palettes, holographic chrome.

## 8. Typography Direction

**Type personality:** Bilingual-first. Confident, contemporary, slightly editorial. *Reads like an art-book imprint that happens to publish ML papers.*

**Chinese (中文) — primary:**
- **Display / Wordmark:** A **modern Heiti with humanist edges** — recommended families: **方正悠黑 (FZ YouHei)**, **思源黑体 Heavy (Source Han Sans)** for system fallback, or a *custom-cut* wordmark for 上海创智学院 / 创智 that subtly references 楷书 stroke endings — giving the heiti a hand-thought "manuscript" trace. Avoid both pure geometric heiti (too cold) and any 宋体 / Songti (too traditional-academic).
- **Body:** **思源宋体 (Source Han Serif) Regular** for long-form editorial pieces (lecture programs, anniversary books) and **思源黑体 Regular** for UI / posters.

**English — primary:**
- **Display / Wordmark:** A **sharp contemporary grotesk with editorial bones** — recommended: **GT America**, **Söhne**, or **ABC Diatype**. *Not* Inter (too generic), *not* Helvetica (too neutral), *not* a tech-startup geometric sans.
- **Editorial / Serif companion:** **GT Sectra** or **Tiempos Headline** — for moments when SII speaks like a journal or a press. Critical for the "intellectual seriousness" register.
- **Monospace (for research/data contexts):** **JetBrains Mono** or **Berkeley Mono** — signals the institute's engineering DNA without overplaying it.

**Bilingual lockup principle:**  
中文 and English must **share optical weight and rhythm**. The Chinese 上海创智学院 and the English Shanghai Innovation Institute (or SII) should look like *one breath*, not two stickers glued together. Recommend tuning Chinese to match the English cap-height optically (not metrically). The Designer should produce a custom-tuned bilingual lockup.

**Type as image:** Encourage **oversized character compositions** — a single 创, 智, or 奇 used at poster-scale as graphic art, paired with small precise English. This is a key visual move for posters, social cards, and environmental graphics.

## 9. Symbol & Mark Strategy

**Three concept directions for the Designer to develop — pick one, push hard:**

### Direction A — 「门 / The Portal」 *(recommended primary direction)*
A geometric **gate/portal mark** that abstracts the radical 门 (door) shared by 院 (academy) and the visual idea of *entering wonderland*. Inside the portal sits a small, precise mark — could be a single dot (the student), a 3-stroke abstraction of 创/智, or a tiny lattice (compute). The portal can be **monogram-locked** with the wordmark below (上海创智学院 / Shanghai Innovation Institute).

- **Strength:** Ownable shape, system-friendly (the portal can frame anything — photos, talks, AI-generated figures), bilingual-neutral, scales from favicon to building-façade signage.
- **Reference touch:** the moon-gate of Suzhou gardens × the framed grid of Beiyang AI Town's glass façade × MIT Media Lab's bracket system.

### Direction B — 「三字印 / The Tri-Glyph Seal」
A modern reinterpretation of the Chinese 印章 (seal) — but cut in clean modular geometry, not faux-classical stone. The seal contains an interlocked composition of **创 · 智 · 奇** (the three character-souls of the institute) reduced to their essential strokes, forming a single symbol. Vermilion on mist-white. Reads as: *modern + unmistakably Chinese + ownable*.

- **Strength:** Strongest cultural anchor; powerful at small sizes; great for institutional documents, certificates, and stamps on merchandise.
- **Risk:** Must avoid any "tourist-Chinese" pastiche. Execution must be *contemporary art* not *calligraphy class*.

### Direction C — 「掰 / The Arm-Wrestle」
Two opposing geometric forms in a moment of locked tension — a literal but abstracted visualization of "一顶一、掰手腕". Reads as *competitive ambition*, energy under load.

- **Strength:** Most distinctive narratively; nothing else in the AI-institute world looks like this.
- **Risk:** May read as aggressive or hard to apply across calm institutional contexts. Best as a **secondary "spirit mark"** alongside Direction A or B.

**Recommendation to Designer:** Lead with **Direction A (Portal)** as the institutional mark. Develop **Direction B (Tri-Glyph Seal)** as a **ceremonial secondary mark** (used on diplomas, anniversary, the building entrance plaque). Develop **Direction C** language only as an **internal/cultural motif** (hoodies, sticker walls, TechFest key visual).

**Wordmark requirements:**
- Custom-tuned **上海创智学院** in modern Heiti, with one or two subtly humanist stroke endings.
- Tightly-paired **Shanghai Innovation Institute** in a sharp grotesk; the abbreviation **SII** should also work standalone as a strong 3-letter monogram.
- Both Chinese and English wordmarks must share a horizontal lockup and a stacked lockup.

## 10. Application Contexts

The system must perform across these touchpoints. Designer should produce at least one hero asset for each tier.

### Tier 1 — Identity Core (Day 1 essentials)
1. **Primary mark** (Portal monogram) + **bilingual wordmark lockup** (horizontal + stacked).
2. **Ceremonial seal** (Direction B) — for diplomas, MOUs, anniversary.
3. **Color & typography specimen sheet** (one-pager that defines the system).

### Tier 2 — Digital
4. **Website hero & navigation** — replacing the current generic template at sii.edu.cn. The portal motif should frame the homepage.
5. **Social cards** — Xiaohongshu / WeChat / 知乎 / LinkedIn / X templates, in both languages.
6. **Lecture poster template** — 创智讲坛 series; should look like a contemporary art-museum poster, not a campus flyer.
7. **Research-paper figure / report cover template** — clean editorial system for the institute's preprints and annual reports.

### Tier 3 — Print & Editorial
8. **Recruitment campaign key visual** — "集不凡·创奇迹 | Calling for Marvellers".
9. **TechFest 创智未来大会 key visual** — annual flagship event identity.
10. **Annual report / anniversary book** — for stakeholders, partners, and government.
11. **Business card / letterhead / envelope** — full stationery suite, bilingual.
12. **Diploma & certificate of completion** — using the ceremonial seal.

### Tier 4 — Environmental
13. **Building façade signage** — the wordmark on the glass curtain wall of Beiyang Building #3, visible from the road.
14. **Interior wayfinding** — floors, lecture halls (1F 多功能厅), labs, mentor offices. Bilingual.
15. **Lecture-hall stage backdrop** — a flexible system that frames talks, panels, demo-days.
16. **Outdoor banners** — for 金秋营, TechFest, open days.

### Tier 5 — Merch & Culture (the *student-love* tier — often skipped, must not be)
17. **Hoodie / T-shirt** with the spirit mark + numerical "PhD cohort" tag.
18. **Lab sticker pack** — including 掰手腕 spirit mark, 创奇 wordmark, lab in-jokes.
19. **Lanyard, tote bag, notebook** — the daily-carry items that turn students into walking ambassadors.
20. **Whiteboard markers / mug / mousepad** — small items that signal "this place takes its tools seriously".

### Tier 6 — Motion & AI-native (the differentiator)
21. **Animated logo / portal opening sequence** — for the AI-concept-film (the institute already produces one, "上海创智学院AI概念片").
22. **Generative key-visual system** — given SII *is* an AI institute, the brand should include a small, opinionated **AI-generated imagery system** with documented prompts/style, so the institute's own students can extend the brand. (This is a unique, ownable move no peer has made.)

## Summary — One Paragraph for the Designer

> Design a bilingual brand identity for **上海创智学院 / Shanghai Innovation Institute (SII)**, a one-year-old, ambitious, founder-flavored AI graduate academy on Shanghai's West Bund. Lead with a **"Portal / 门" geometric monogram** that frames the institute as a wonderland to *enter*, paired with a custom bilingual wordmark in modern Heiti + sharp grotesk. The palette is **West-Bund-ink near-black, mist-white, compute-grey, and a decisive Chinese vermilion (`#E94B1F`)** used at ~5%. Type as image — oversized 创/智/奇 characters at poster scale. A ceremonial **tri-glyph seal** serves diplomas and the building plaque. Avoid every cliché of "AI + innovation" branding: no circuit boards, no blue-gradients, no neural-glow, no Latin mottos. The brand should feel like **a contemporary Shanghai art museum that happens to train people who will arm-wrestle the future of AI** — restrained, confident, slightly mischievous, unmistakably Chinese-modern, and ready for the next thirty years.
