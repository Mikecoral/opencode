## Batch rationale

- The mandatory `logo-primary` anchors the system around the brief’s preferred “SII 三段式开放链路”.
- The first batch prioritizes assets that unlock many downstream applications: logo lockup, palette, typography, graphic primitives, and poster templates.
- Poster-ready assets are intentionally template-like and placeholder-driven so future complex posters can insert verified data from the poster data bank without hallucinated claims.
- Digital, badge, and signage mockups are included because the brief identifies website, admissions/camps, PPT/events, and spatial wayfinding as high-frequency touchpoints.
