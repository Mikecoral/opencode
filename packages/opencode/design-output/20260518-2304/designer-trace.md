### Why this final 4-8?
The final eight assets cover both foundational identity and the highest-priority public touchpoints: institutional recognition, digital publishing, admissions, flagship events, campus space, and open-source research dissemination. This mix reflects SII as a new research-oriented AI talent institution and “scientist-maker community,” not a conventional college or commercial AI startup. Lower-priority but still relevant items—PPT, recruitment-only graphics, and merchandise—are deferred because they can be derived from the same grid, color, and template system after the core visual language is validated.

### Decisions & Tradeoffs
The prompt_search calls were attempted for every planned asset but the tool consistently failed with `Effect.catchAll is not a function`; I proceeded from scratch rather than blocking generation, and documented the failed searches above. For text-heavy bilingual assets, I included exact English and limited Chinese text because these touchpoints require institutional naming, while recognizing that generated Chinese typography may need manual production refinement. An initial imagegen batch using `design-output/20260518-2304/<filename>` was saved by the tool into a nested `design-output/design-output/...` path because the tool automatically prefixes outputs with `design-output/`; I regenerated the final reviewed set using `20260518-2304/<filename>` so the files land at the requested `design-output/20260518-2304/` paths.
