# Asset Generation Plan

## Rationale
Shanghai Innovation Institute / 创智学院 is an organization at the intersection of AI research, talent cultivation, open-source collaboration, events, and campus space. The planned assets prioritize the brief’s Section 11 touchpoints: official web/research communication, admissions camps, TechFest, spatial signage, open-source research dissemination, recruiting, and community merchandise—while anchoring everything in an SII intelligent grid rather than generic “AI education” imagery.
