# Brand Strategy Brief: 创智学院

Generated: 2026-05-19

## 1. Brand Context

创智学院 is positioned as a future-facing learning institution that connects creativity, intelligence, technology, and human growth. The identity should feel academic enough to be trusted, but not traditional or bureaucratic. It should avoid generic school crests, book icons, lightbulbs, and graduation caps.

## 2. Brand Positioning

Position 创智学院 as a cross-disciplinary innovation academy: a place where learners turn curiosity into intelligent creation. The brand should sit between an education institute, an innovation lab, and a design-tech studio.

## 3. Audience

- Primary: students, young professionals, makers, and founders seeking creative technology education.
- Secondary: parents, partner schools, enterprises, and education-sector collaborators.
- Tertiary: faculty, mentors, investors, and public-sector innovation programs.

## 4. Brand Personality

- Clear, intelligent, and structured.
- Warm, optimistic, and growth-oriented.
- Experimental but disciplined.
- International, modern, and culturally rooted.

## 5. Core Idea

The visual system is built around the idea of an "intelligence spark matrix": individual points of insight connect into a structured learning network, forming a rising path from knowledge to invention.

## 6. Symbol Direction

The primary mark should combine three ideas without becoming literal:

- A spark or seed of creation.
- A modular grid or neural network of intelligence.
- An upward open doorway or path suggesting learning progression.

The mark should work as a simple geometric icon at small sizes and as a flexible pattern system at large sizes.

## 7. Color Strategy

- Primary: deep academy navy, representing trust, knowledge, and depth.
- Accent: electric cyan, representing intelligence, digital systems, and clarity.
- Warm accent: sunrise amber, representing creativity, optimism, and human energy.
- Neutral: porcelain white and graphite gray for clean academic applications.

Suggested palette:

- Academy Navy: #10243E
- Intelligence Cyan: #21C7E8
- Creative Amber: #FFB23F
- Porcelain: #F7F3EA
- Graphite: #2A2F36

## 8. Typography Direction

Chinese typography should use a modern sans-serif with humanist proportions, similar in spirit to 思源黑体 / Noto Sans SC. Latin typography should pair with a geometric sans such as Inter, Avenir Next, or Sora. Use confident medium and semibold weights. Avoid playful handwritten fonts.

## 9. Graphic System

Use modular nodes, intelligent paths, semi-transparent grids, and cropped geometric sparks. Patterns should feel like learning networks, not abstract fintech decoration. Layouts should include strong white space, clear hierarchy, and precise alignment.

- Typography specimen.
- Brand application mockup.
