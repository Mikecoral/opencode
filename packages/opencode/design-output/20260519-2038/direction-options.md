# Direction Options

## 1. Intelligence Spark Matrix

A precise geometric mark made from connected nodes and an upward spark aperture. Deep navy, cyan, amber, and porcelain. Best for a modern academy with technology and creativity at its core.

## 2. Future Courtyard

A softer identity inspired by a campus courtyard, using open rectangular frames, warm light, and layered learning spaces. More institutional and welcoming, less tech-forward.

## 3. Maker Constellation

A playful but premium identity built from constellation-like maker tools, modules, and learning paths. More energetic and youth-oriented, slightly less formal.

## Selected Direction
