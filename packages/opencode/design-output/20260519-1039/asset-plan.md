# Asset Generation Plan

## Rationale
This run should translate Shanghai Innovation Institute into a white-first AI research operating system, not a conventional university package. The approved `signal-glass-grid` option directly fits the brief’s “前沿信号,” modular AI research fields, node-based collaboration model, and the user’s preference for white, mist silver, cool gray, glass layers, and restrained signal accents.

## Visual Direction
- Selected: research-signal
- Direction variant: approved Option 1, `signal-glass-grid`
- Why: The brief repeatedly foregrounds frontier signals, AI research fields, open-source iteration, mentor/industry networks, and high-density research communication. Direction Options recommends `signal-glass-grid` as the best light visual-system route, with modular signal grids, translucent panels, thin key lines, and white / mist-silver layering.
- What this prevents: Avoids the predictable education default of crest, campus hero, admissions brochure, and red/blue academic palette; also avoids heavy dark cyberpunk AI branding and generic neural-network wallpaper.

## Deliverable Strategy
- Selected: digital-system
- Why: Section 11’s top priorities are information systems and public communication surfaces: admissions camp / summer-camp information, research-result publishing, event visuals, and global talent recruitment pages or posters. A digital-system strategy can make these feel like one coherent SII interface rather than separate campaigns.

- Did you avoid defaulting to color-palette/campus/admissions/brochure/merchandise? Yes — color-palette, campus, brochure, and merchandise assets are rejected; admissions appears only because Section 11 ranks it Top priority and it is treated as an information-system template rather than a generic admissions campaign.
- Does the set feel like one coherent system rather than unrelated samples? Yes — every asset uses the same white/mist-silver glass surfaces, cool-gray hierarchy, modular signal grid, and single primary cyan signal accent.
