## Strengths
The brief is unusually well grounded for a first-stage strategy document. It ties SII’s official mission, location, audience, research agenda, and public communications to specific design implications. The strongest sections are Cultural & Visual DNA, Symbol & Mark Strategy, and Application Contexts: they give the designer concrete motifs and priority deliverables without defaulting to a generic university admissions package.
