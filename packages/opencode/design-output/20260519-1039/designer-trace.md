# Designer Trace

## Phase 1: Asset Planning

### Subject Type & Brief Touchpoints
Subject Type quoted from brief: **organization**.

Relevant Section 11 classification:
- 招生营 / 夏令营信息系统与报名视觉 — Top priority: high-frequency public admissions entry point with doctoral selection notices, exam outlines, and computer-based test manuals.
- 科研成果发布模板（网页、长图、论文/开源项目卡片） — Top priority: ongoing AI Infra, robotics, large-model, open-source, and research-output publication needs.
- 创智讲坛 / 大师报告 / TechFest 活动主视觉 — Top priority: major lectures and academic reports are key windows for external recognition.
- 人才引进 / 青年教师全球招聘页面与海报 — Top priority: official homepage promotes global young-faculty recruitment and “Calling for Marvellers.”
- 五大科研阵地图形系统 — Optional: strategically important but brief says to define core identity rules first.
- 空间导视与环境图形 — Optional: long-term need for the 27,000 sqm independent space, but not the first digital communication run.
- 通用文创周边 — Not this round: lower value for current admissions, research, and talent-introduction needs.

### Visual Direction Decision
- Selected direction: research-signal, using the approved Option 1 variant `signal-glass-grid`.
- Alternatives considered: institutional-minimal, campaign-energy, spatial-system.
- Why selected: The brief’s differentiators are AI frontier signals, five research fields, open-source iteration, mentor/industry collaboration, and research-to-value pathways. The user explicitly approved a white/light visual system with mist silver, cool gray, glass layers, modular signal grid, and restrained accents.
- Visual research used: Direction Options recommends `signal-glass-grid`; Visual References emphasize MIT Media Lab’s generative-grid logic, IBM Carbon’s light gray layering and 2x grid discipline, OpenAI’s white-space restraint, and research-card architectures from DeepMind / IBM Research.
- Generic defaults avoided: traditional education crest, admissions + campus + brochure bundle, red/blue/gold university palette, dark AI cyberpunk hero, generic neural-network wallpaper, stock scientist imagery.

### Deliverable Strategy Decision
- Selected strategy: digital-system.
- Why selected: Section 11’s top-priority needs are mainly information and publication surfaces: admissions information, research result cards, lecture/event visuals, and talent-recruitment web or poster modules.
- Why other strategies were rejected or deferred: `identity-core` alone would under-serve urgent top-priority communication templates; `campaign-system` would overemphasize events and recruitment at the expense of research publishing; `spatial-system` is Optional; `publishing-system` fits research releases but not admissions and talent recruitment broadly enough; `community-system` and `product-packaging` are not supported by Section 11.

### Why this final 4-8?
The final six assets form a compact digital identity system: one symbol, one grid grammar board, and four top-priority communication templates. Omitted defaults such as color-palette, campus hero, generic brochure, and merchandise were not generated because they would dilute the approved signal-glass-grid direction and recreate a predictable education bundle rather than SII’s research-signal operating system.

### Decisions & Tradeoffs
Generation preserved the approved `signal-glass-grid` direction and the user's white-based preference. To reconcile the user’s mention of restrained cyan/violet/green accents with `brand-tokens.md` accent discipline, prompts used Signal Cyan as the only active accent and allowed only faint glass refraction rather than active violet or green palette tokens. All six image requests failed due to an authentication issue outside the design prompt layer: OpenAI API HTTP 401 invalid token. No assets were silently skipped; every planned filename was attempted in the required `20260519-1039/<filename>` format.
