# Visual References

## Brief Inputs Used
Research was shaped by the brief sections on Cultural & Visual DNA, Color Strategy, Typography Direction, Symbol & Mark Strategy, and Application Contexts, plus the critique warning that the palette can become overextended. The user constraint was treated as decisive: options must be white/light-first, precise, clean, glass/silver based, with cyan/violet/green only as restrained signal accents; avoid traditional university red/blue/gold and heavy dark cyberpunk.

## References
| Reference | Source URL | Why relevant | Visual moves to borrow | What to avoid |
|-----------|------------|--------------|------------------------|---------------|
| MIT Media Lab identity by Pentagram | https://www.pentagram.com/work/mit-media-lab/story | Education + technology research lab with multiple research groups needing one system. | A single underlying geometric grid can generate a parent mark and field/group glyphs; simple sans typography gives the variable symbols authority; wayfinding and identity are treated as one modular language. | Do not imitate the 7×7 ML form or rainbow block personality; SII needs cooler, lighter, more glass/infrastructure specific output. |
| IBM Carbon Design System: Color + 2x Grid | https://carbondesignsystem.com/elements/color/overview/ and https://carbondesignsystem.com/elements/2x-grid/overview/ | Strong model for white/gray interface systems that can handle dense research, admissions, dashboards, and data. | White and Gray 10 layering; neutral gray dominance; one primary action/signal color; role-based tokens; 8px/2x mini-unit rhythm; visible key lines for dense information. | Avoid looking like an IBM product UI or default enterprise SaaS; SII needs a custom mark, bilingual hierarchy, and more campus/research atmosphere. |
| IBM Design Language | https://www.ibm.com/design/language/ | Mature technical organization language spanning type, color, grid, icons, illustration, photography, data visualization, and animation. | Treat brand as a complete operating system: typography, diagrams, data visualization, iconography, photography, and motion all need rules; use technical diagrams and line-based illustration as primary assets. | Avoid corporate heaviness, IBM blue ownership cues, or over-standardized B2B coldness. |
| OpenAI Design Guidelines | https://openai.com/brand/ | Contemporary AI research/deployment brand using white space, black typography, minimal marks, and light-background collateral. | Large open white fields; black/charcoal wordmark confidence; friendly geometric sans; research paper covers with pastel accents; strict clear space; no effects/textures on core marks. | Avoid copying circular blossom geometry or becoming too consumer/lifestyle; SII needs institutional credibility and a more modular research-field system. |
| Google DeepMind site / research architecture | https://deepmind.google/about/ | AI lab presentation that organizes models, research, science, responsibility, careers, and news in a bright, card-based system. | White page architecture, product/research cards, soft scientific imagery, multiple research streams handled through consistent tiles, clear mission headline with calm confidence. | Avoid Google-like multicolor gradients and generic AI website cards; SII should not inherit Google product-brand warmth or primary-color palette. |
| IBM Research website | https://www.research.ibm.com/ | Research publication and open-source project hub with frontier science, news cards, tags, code/tools, and technical imagery. | High-density news/research cards; taxonomy tags; code/tool project tiles; sharp headline-to-metadata hierarchy; mix of scientific imagery and interface-like structure. | Avoid dark hero dependence and IBM brand codes; borrow the publishing logic, not the color identity. |
