# Brand Tokens: 上海创智学院（Shanghai Innovation Institute / SII）

## Color Palette
| Role | Name | Hex | Usage |
|------|------|-----|-------|
| Primary | Deep Space Ink Blue | #07111F | Logo ground, website hero, research pages, report-cover base |
| Accent | SII Electric Cyan | #00E0FF | Signal paths, data nodes, CTAs, active states, event highlights |
| Surface | West Bund Silver | #E7EBF0 | Light UI panels, cards, diagrams, accessible backgrounds |
| Muted | Graphite Gray | #2B313A | Secondary text, structural grid lines, subdued interface elements |

**Accent discipline:** one accent color only. Human warmth from the brief should be expressed through spacing, rounded geometry, and approachable typography rather than adding a second active accent in this run.

## Typography
| Role | Style Description | Weight Range |
|------|-------------------|-------------|
| Display / Logo | Narrow, rational modern Chinese sans with open counters; humanist-geometric Latin sans with engineering precision and slight rounded warmth | Bold / Black |
| Body | Clear humanist-geometric sans optimized for long-form research content and bilingual digital interfaces | Regular / Medium |
| Caption | Compact sans with high legibility for data labels, project metadata, and interface captions | Light / Regular |

## Design Keywords (from brief Section 7)
AI frontier zone, young PI, open-source infrastructure, project-based research, industry-academia-research-venture ecosystem, generative grid, West Bund night voyage, impossible to public benefit, scientist-maker community, human-AI co-creation

## Cultural Motifs (from brief Section 5)
AI frontier network of nodes, connections, and emergence; creation plus intelligence expressed through S/I/I or abstracted 创/智 structure; North Yang AI Town and Xuhui West Bund riverfront context with glass curtain walls and night laboratories; generative grid identity system for research fronts; three-stage path from impossible to possible to public benefit
