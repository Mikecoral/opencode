## Recommendation Derivation

### Recommendation 1: Make the primary mark more ownable and less generic-network
- Triggered by: `logo-primary.png` scored only 6 for subject specificity and had ambiguous S/I/I legibility.
- Why this fix: A stronger primary mark will make the entire system less generic and give the subbrand grid a clearer origin.
- How the imagegen prompt addresses it: It requires a strict 7x7 grid, lower node density, explicit three-node progression, and scalable negative-space S/I/I logic.

### Recommendation 2: Replace generic AI atmosphere with SII-specific research evidence
- Triggered by: `website-research-hero.png` was praised for hierarchy but criticized for glowing neural meshes, futuristic architecture, and broad AI terms.
- Why this fix: The digital-system strategy should privilege repeatable research evidence and SII operations over cinematic AI mood.
- How the imagegen prompt addresses it: It swaps decorative neural glow for five research-front glyphs, partner metrics, and the impossible-to-public-benefit progress path.

### Recommendation 3: Edit campaign density and improve practical legibility
- Triggered by: `techfest-open-day-key-visual.png` had strong energy but low restraint due to HUDs, devices, grids, cityscape, lab details, and microcopy.
- Why this fix: The poster needs to work at real viewing distance and feel premium/institutional rather than AI-generated spectacle.
- How the imagegen prompt addresses it: It limits the composition to one grid tunnel, one three-node path, larger text, fewer elements, and more negative space.
