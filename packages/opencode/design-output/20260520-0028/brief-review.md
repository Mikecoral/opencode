# Brief Review: 上海创智学院 / Shanghai Innovation Institute

## Overall Brief Quality: 8/10

## Section Scores
| Section | Score | Issue |
|---------|-------|-------|
| Design Keywords | 8/10 | Strong strategic language, but several terms remain broad institutional/AI descriptors rather than image-specific cues. |

## Flagged Issues
- **Design Keywords:** Terms like “AI基础设施,” “跨界协同,” and “公共可信度” are strategically accurate but not all visually vivid. Suggested rewrite: “modular intelligence grid, West Bund research infrastructure, central node with open endpoints, bilingual research-engineering authority, precise cyan signal on dark institutional substrate.”
- **Color Strategy:** The palette’s cyan + violet pairing could easily become generic AI gradient language. Suggested rewrite: make **Midnight Navy + Cool White + Graphite** the 80–90% base, use **Intelligence Cyan** as the single primary signal color, reserve **Data Violet** only for secondary research categories or motion states, and explicitly ban cyan-violet gradient hero art.
- **Symbol & Mark:** The brief lists three plausible mark concepts but does not force a primary direction. Suggested rewrite: prioritize the **SII modular lettermark built from an 8×8 grid** with one central “hub” cell and two vertical “institute” columns; allow “创/智” glyph abstractions only as secondary exploration.
- **Typography:** The section names many acceptable fonts, which may lead to default sans-serif sameness. Suggested rewrite: specify one preferred bilingual system: “Chinese: Source Han Sans SC / Noto Sans SC Medium-Semibold for headings and Regular for body; English: IBM Plex Sans for headings/body; IBM Plex Mono only for codes, data labels, and course identifiers.”

## Recommendation
PROCEED — The brief is substantially imagegen-ready and strategically differentiated; tighten color hierarchy, mark priority, and font pairing in prompts to avoid generic AI-institution output.
