## Shared Creative Constraints

- Dominant base: Midnight Navy `#071A2D`, Cool White `#F6F9FC`, Graphite `#2B3440` / `#7E8A99`.
- Primary signal: Intelligence Cyan `#00B8FF`.
- Data Violet `#6B5CFF` reserved only for secondary research categories or motion states.
- Core mark: SII modular lettermark built from an 8x8 grid with one central hub cell and two vertical institute columns.
- Typography direction: Source Han Sans SC / Noto Sans SC style for Chinese, IBM Plex Sans style for English, IBM Plex Mono style only for data or course labels.
- Explicit exclusions: glowing brain, robot mascot, code rain, random neural sphere, book, graduation cap, lightbulb, rocket, traditional seal, generic cyan-violet gradient hero art.

## Prompting Approach

The assets were generated directly with `gpt-image-2` after the designer subagent unexpectedly declined generation. Prompts were adapted from the approved `brief.md`, `brief-review.md`, and `asset-plan.md`, with emphasis on a disciplined Intelligence Grid identity system for a public-facing AI talent and research institute.
