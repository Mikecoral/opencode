# Asset Plan: 上海创智学院 / Shanghai Innovation Institute
Design goal: Establish a disciplined “Intelligence Grid” identity system for a national/city-level AI talent and research platform, led by an 8×8 modular SII lettermark, dark institutional substrate, precise cyan signal behavior, and bilingual research-engineering typography.
