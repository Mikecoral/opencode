# Direction Options — 创智学院

## Direction 1

**Direction Name:** 智能矩阵 / Intelligence Matrix  
**Strategic rationale:** Fits an academy positioned around AI, innovation, and systematic capability-building.  
**Visual register:** Technical authority + precise engineering.  
**Color logic:** Deep ink navy `#0A1628` + electric blue near IBM core `#0F62FE` + cool gray `#F2F4F8`; optional cyan accent `#00CCF5` for data / AI states.  
**Typography logic:** IBM Plex Sans Chinese SC / IBM Plex Sans-style technical sans for Chinese-Latin consistency; optional mono numerals for course codes and systems diagrams.  
**Mark concept:** Modular grid/path mark where “创” and “智” are abstracted into connected blocks or signal paths; system can expand into sub-marks for programs.  
**Key reference:** MIT Media Lab identity by Pentagram — seven-by-seven grid generating a family of glyphs.  
**What to avoid:** Do not become generic “blue tech logo + circuit board” with no distinctive Chinese-language structure.

## Direction 2

**Direction Name:** 点燃创造 / Spark of Creation  
**Strategic rationale:** Positions 创智学院 as an energetic place where learners turn ideas into action, not just receive credentials.  
**Visual register:** Optimistic learning + active experimentation.  
**Color logic:** Warm orange `#FF9408` + vivid magenta `#EC0089` + cyan `#00CCF5` on white/near-black; use color as “spark / node / pathway” accents rather than full-surface chaos.  
**Typography logic:** Rounded humanist sans for warmth, with bold Chinese display moments for slogans and program launches.  
**Mark concept:** A spark/node emerging from an open book, cursor, or seed-like learner symbol; motion/extension creates learning paths.  
**Key reference:** DeepLearning.AI hero system — vivid network graphics and “spark” metaphor.  
**What to avoid:** Do not overuse neon gradients or cartoon AI motifs that make the academy feel juvenile.

## Direction 3

**Direction Name:** 创新工坊 / Creative Lab Campus  
**Strategic rationale:** Best if the brand should emphasize interdisciplinary problem-solving, workshops, design thinking, and community.  
**Visual register:** Human-centered creativity + contemporary academic culture.  
**Color logic:** Warm off-white `#FAF7F0` + charcoal `#161616` + Stanford-like red/orange accent `#D83A20` + soft teal `#14BF96`; tactile, less corporate than blue edtech.  
**Typography logic:** Strong CJK sans for clarity paired with expressive italic/variable Latin accents; editorial hierarchy with large verbs and workshop labels.  
**Mark concept:** Flexible “workbench” mark: interlocking shapes that can read as book pages, tools, dialogue, and idea blocks.  
**Key reference:** Stanford d.school website — textured panels, expressive typography, resource-card ecosystem.  
**What to avoid:** Do not become messy maker-culture collage; keep a clear academic spine and consistent system.

## Supplement — Audience Lens for Existing Directions

Evidence base: official 上海创智学院 overview / admissions pages and media reports indicate the primary audience is doctoral-level AI “researcher-founder” talent, not broad K12 or mass AI-literacy learners. Secondary audiences include co-building universities, government / education authorities, enterprise partners, mentors, investors, and media.

### Direction 1 — 智能矩阵 / Intelligence Matrix

**Audience fit evidence:** Strong fit for documented doctoral / AI Infra / research-infrastructure audience. Official and media sources emphasize math, coding, 48-hour practical projects, AI Infra, large-scale compute, 10PB data platform, and frontier technical systems.  
**Audience trust need:** Must make the new, flexible model feel academically rigorous and officially credible.  
**Application priority from audience research:** admissions selection materials, technical reports, research launch pages, infrastructure diagrams, partner / government presentations.  
**Audience risk:** If overly cold or generic, it may underrepresent the documented “researcher-founder,” entrepreneurship, and autonomy motivations.

### Direction 2 — 点燃创造 / Spark of Creation

**Audience fit evidence:** Fits the documented “闯劲,” risk tolerance, “从不可能到可能,” creator / founder energy, maker competitions, camps, and startup incubation.  
**Audience trust need:** Because the institution is new and nontraditional, expressive energy needs visible academic / technical discipline.  
**Application priority from audience research:** open day / TechFest materials, demo days, student founder stories, cohort recruitment campaigns, project showcase motion graphics.  
**Audience risk:** Too much consumer-edtech friendliness or neon “AI hype” would conflict with doctoral-level selectivity and institutional stakeholder credibility.

### Direction 3 — 创新工坊 / Creative Lab Campus

**Audience fit evidence:** Fits cross-disciplinary examples such as sociology-to-AI problem solving, student-led PI projects, mentor groups, seminars, and “scientist maker community” language.  
**Audience trust need:** Needs enough elite research / AI specificity so it does not read as a general design-thinking school.  
**Application priority from audience research:** campus / lab environment graphics, seminar systems, interdisciplinary project pages, student collaboration storytelling, recruitment for nontraditional candidates.  
**Audience risk:** If too workshop-like or undergraduate-campus oriented, it may miss the documented doctoral / researcher-founder maturity level.

### Cross-direction audience constraints

- Visual maturity level should skew **graduate research institute / venture lab**, not youth education, coding bootcamp, or corporate training.
- Cues to preserve across any direction: official institutional credibility, advanced AI research, student autonomy, high-density mentor / peer ecosystem, and startup / translation pathway.
- Cues to avoid across any direction: cartoon robots, K12 STEM colors, mass-course marketplace cards, stock classroom photography, and generic “AI blue gradient” without evidence of research depth.
