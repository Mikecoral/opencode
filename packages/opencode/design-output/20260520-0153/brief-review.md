# Brief Review: 创智学院

## Overall Brief Quality: 8/10

## Section Scores
| Section | Score | Issue |
|---------|-------|-------|
| Design Keywords | 8/10 | Strong strategic language, but several terms are abstract concepts rather than directly image-generative cues. |
| Visual Direction | 9/10 | Clear and distinctive “intelligence matrix” system with useful anti-patterns; highly usable for visual generation. |
| Color Strategy | 8/10 | Specific hex values and usage contexts are included, but IBM blue risks feeling borrowed/generic unless proportions and gradient restrictions are tightened. |
| Symbol & Mark | 8/10 | Good conceptual territory and clear taboos, but it still offers multiple possible mark routes; choosing one construction would produce more distinctive output. |
| Typography | 7/10 | Solid category guidance, yet the font list is broad and could default to generic tech sans; needs a more decisive pairing and typographic behavior. |

## Flagged Issues
- **Design Keywords:** Terms like “系统生成,” “高密度协同,” and “前沿基础设施” are strategically meaningful but may generate generic node-network graphics unless paired with concrete form language.  
  **Suggested rewrite:** “7×7 research matrix, activated node paths, dark institutional lab system, modular AI institute grid, CZ stroke-path mark, high-density mentor / compute / data network, mono-coded coordinates, restrained electric signal highlights.”

- **Color Strategy:** The palette is specific, but `#0F62FE` plus cyan on near-black can still become a familiar enterprise-tech / AI dashboard look.  
  **Suggested rewrite:** “Use near-black blue `#0A1628` and off-white / blue-gray neutrals for 80% of the system; reserve `#0F62FE` for one primary path or CTA per composition; use `#00CCF5` only for active nodes / data pulses; use `#FF9408` only for Demo Day or entrepreneurship moments, never as a general accent; avoid purple-blue gradients and full-bleed neon glow.”

- **Symbol & Mark:** The mark concept is promising but still branches into three options. Image generation may produce a generic grid, hexagon, or abstract tech icon unless the construction is narrowed.  
  **Suggested rewrite:** “Create a compact symbol from a 5×5 or 7×7 square matrix: two angular paths, derived from the stroke logic of ‘创’ and ‘智’ / C and Z, intersect at one activated node. The mark must read as an institutional seal at small size, work in one color, and avoid circuit-board traces, chips, books, bulbs, caps, or robots.”

- **Typography:** The direction names acceptable font families but does not yet specify hierarchy or contrast between institutional and system-information layers.  
  **Suggested rewrite:** “Use IBM Plex Sans Chinese / Source Han Sans as the main institutional voice, paired with IBM Plex Mono for coordinates, program IDs, dates, exam codes, and system labels. Headlines should be heavy, compact, and slightly tight; uppercase English labels should be tracked out; mono text should appear as a secondary data layer, not as the primary brand voice.”

## Recommendation
PROCEED — The brief is unusually specific and strategically differentiated; tighten the mark construction and typographic hierarchy during generation to avoid generic AI-grid outcomes.
