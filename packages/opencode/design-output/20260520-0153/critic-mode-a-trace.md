Assessment: The typography section is credible and appropriate for a bilingual AI research institute. ## Scoring Rationale
The overall score is 8/10, based on strong strategy specificity and a highly usable visual system. Section average is 8.0. Visual Direction is excellent and raises the brief above generic AI education branding. Typography and Symbol & Mark are the main opportunities for refinement because they need more decisive execution rules to prevent generic tech-sans and node-grid outcomes.
